About /

Christian. Husband. Father. Feminist, increasingly socialist, always anti-fascist. Antipodean immigrant in the UK. Cinephile. Jack-of-all email marketing trades. Board game & RPG enthusiast. Stroke survivor.

Email /

I've worked in email since before Gmail existed. Literally.

My mum ran one of Australia's first email service providers, and I helped manage campaigns from her home office. That's where I learned the craft.

Since then I've gathered more than 18 years of experience across strategy, testing, design, deliverability, and copy. These days I co-lead Holistic Email Marketing and Holistic Email Academy with my mum, Kath Pay. Between us we cover what email actually requires: strategy, testing, deliverability, copywriting, and training.

Email gets reduced to tools and metrics more often than it should. I care about the writing as much as the data.

The detail of what I do day to day is on the work page. I also build small tools for my own use and put them on the tools page for anyone who wants them.

Writing /

I write about work, faith, life, and fun. Some of it is backed up here from places it first appeared: a Tumblr from years back, a Substack, and the WordPress site this one replaces.

My faith shapes my politics, and my politics aren't what a lot of Christians expect. I read the Bible as something other than a rulebook. I'm a feminist inside a tradition that has spent a long time arguing about women preaching. None of that sits tidily next to a career in email, and I've stopped trying to make it.

If you want the reasoning behind that, start with More Than One Thing.

Elsewhere /

I'm Australian, living in the UK, in Weston-super-Mare. I survived a stroke. I watch too many films and play too many board games and RPGs. I'm a father, and my marriage is ending, which I've written about only briefly so far. My kids didn't sign up for a blog, so I'll keep them out of it.

The easiest way to reach me is on LinkedIn or Bluesky. If you'd like a clear head on your email programme, tell me what you're working on. No pitch, just a conversation.
