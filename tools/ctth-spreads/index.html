The deck

Thirty-six cards: four for each of the nine Icons, numbered 11 to 66 like a d66. The colours run green, blue, ochre and red down each Icon's four.

Each card has a short gloss. Read it against its position: the same card reads differently as the Surface than as the Secret.

Saved NPCs

Spreads you've kept, set Icon first. The Owed card can name another saved NPC, so debts and favours link your cast together.

Your card images

The page draws its own cards. To use your own scans instead, choose them here. Choose the folder of scans, or the files. Each one is matched to a card by the number in its name (11 to 66, so 43 Gambler.png), or failing that by the card's title (the-gambler-debt.jpg). A file with back in its name becomes the card back.

They stay in this browser and are never uploaded.
