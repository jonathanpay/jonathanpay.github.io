System

An unofficial system chart and world generator for Coriolis: The Third Horizon. The jobs, and the trip between systems, live in the Mission tool.

No data file loaded, so the charts show the map's own counts and the world tables show rolls and table numbers rather than results.

Find a system

The map carries the Horizon's 36 systems and the portal links between them. Your data file, your own list and any system saved to the campaign join it here, and a campaign system brings its bodies with it.

Roll a world here

The Worlds tab rolls a planet, gas giant or asteroid belt for the system you have open.

Generate a world

Modifiers chain as you go: atmosphere moves temperature and geosphere, size moves population, population moves the spaceport. Rerolling a line reworks the lines below it.

The log

Worlds you save, with their tables, their hooks and their factions. Copy any entry as Markdown for your notes. Jobs and voyages are logged in the Mission tool.

An unofficial fan tool. It is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Free League Publishing (Fria Ligan AB). Coriolis: The Third Horizon and its names are theirs. No rules text, table entries, art or setting text from the books is included: the page carries dice, table numbers and place names, and anything more comes from a data file you make and keep yourself. What you save stays in this browser.
