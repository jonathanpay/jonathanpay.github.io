Atlas

An unofficial map maker for Coriolis: The Third Horizon. Start from a blank page: place systems, link them by portals, build out their worlds, and save the lot to your campaign.

    Build out a system

    Name each star, set where the portals sit, then add its bodies with their distances in AD. Open "World details" on a body for its size, atmosphere, population and hooks. The chart draws as you type.

    Rolling worlds

    Once a map is saved to the campaign, its systems show in the System tool, where the Worlds tab rolls a planet, gas giant or asteroid belt for any of them from your own tables, and in Coriolis Sheets.

    The map has no systems yet. Add some on the Map tab.
