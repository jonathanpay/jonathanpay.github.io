Tools /

Design Tools /

Small helpers for the content I make over and over. The Journaling Bible Generator and the Kintsugi Overlay Generator are built specifically for my Living the Scandalous Life Substack; the Carousel Maker is for social posts, and Paste-up is for collage. A vibe coding exercise, so expect rough edges. They run in your browser, and nothing you load leaves it.

Design

Carousel Maker

Build social media carousel slides in seconds. Four template families, ten layouts, two formats — export as PNG, no install needed.
Design

Journaling Bible Generator

Lay a passage out the way a journaling Bible does: scripture down two thirds of the page, handwritten marginalia in the third beside it. Exports a 3000 × 3000 PNG.
Design

Kintsugi Overlay Generator

Draw bold gold lines and shapes over a black and white photo, pick the exact gold, and export the flattened PNG.
Design

Paste-up

Cut out public-domain art and openly licensed photos, arrange them into a collage, and export a PNG with every source image's credit attached.

Tabletop Tools /

Prep tools for the games I run. Unofficial and unaffiliated: bring your own books. They run in your browser, and what you save stays in it.

Coriolis: The Great Dark

Voyage Chart Table

Plot Slipstream routes across the Charted Sphere, plan each week's hazards, events and crew duties, then run the voyage at the table with Hull and Morale tracked.
Coriolis: The Great Dark

Delve Table

Roll a ruin through the whole delve-creation chain, paint its map on a grid and place its points, then run the descent: supply, hazards, scans, camps, shards, artefacts and a log. Show the players a deep-scan handout of only what a scan could reach, and send it all to your campaign.
Coriolis: The Great Dark

Great Dark Sheets

Editable Explorer, ship, NPC, delve and voyage log sheets. Health, Hope and Heart work themselves out, supply is a row of boxes you tick, ships read Hull, Morale and upgrades from the Voyage Chart Table, and everything prints clean in A4 landscape.
Coriolis: The Third Horizon

Mission Board

Post jobs for any group concept, and roll the trip there leg by leg with encounters, jumps and misfortunes. The system and the world at the end of it are charted in the System tool.
Coriolis: The Third Horizon

System

Chart every system on the Horizon map: its stars, its bodies in AD and its portal links, campaign systems included. Roll a planet, gas giant or asteroid belt for any of them and save it to the campaign.
Coriolis: The Third Horizon

Atlas

Make your own map from a blank page: place star systems, link them with portals, flag unstable portals and dangerous areas, build out each system's worlds in the same holographic style, and save the lot to your campaign.
Coriolis: The Third Horizon

Coriolis Sheets

Editable character, spaceship, system, mission and NPC sheets. Generate from tables, start with ship models and NPC archetypes, save your campaign and print clean copies. Shares one campaign with the Mission, System and Icon Readings tools.

Read the Icon deck like a tarot spread. Five cards build an NPC, or find the twist in a job you have already rolled from the books' tables, with a third reading for the shape of a story.
