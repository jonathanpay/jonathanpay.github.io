Cut from copy

  • delve into / deep dive
  • in today's world…
  • let's dive in
  • underscore (as verb)
  • serves as (where "is" works)
  • not just X, but also Y
  • it's not X, it's Y
  • crucial / pivotal / notable
  • quietly fades / quiet [emotion]
  • journey (unless literal)

Use instead

  • Yet…
  • Except…
  • Which is where…
  • Then suddenly…
  • And honestly…
  • Actually…
  • Which is a different thing entirely.
