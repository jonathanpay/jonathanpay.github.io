Last night was something special—Dotdigital’s 25th anniversary, celebrated with a silver edition of the Dotties. Held at The Shard, it was an exclusive, pared-back event for finalists, judges, sponsors, and Dotdigital’s team. Formal dress, close company, and a stunning view of London set the scene.

Judging this year’s entries was eye-opening. The work submitted reflects just how far email marketing has come. Each campaign was a testament to the creativity and precision driving our field forward. Seeing finalists and winners recognised in person was a rare moment of pride in this industry.

Dotdigital went all out for this milestone. As an attendee, it was a privilege to witness such dedication to celebrating innovation and resilience in email marketing. Here’s to the next 25 years.
