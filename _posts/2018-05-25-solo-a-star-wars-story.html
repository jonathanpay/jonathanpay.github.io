It was more than likely an assistant, however it was a very cool moment to receive a quote tweet from famed Hollywood director, Ron Howard!

I did enjoy Solo. They made a really fun story. My major critique however, was that they tried to fit too many nods and references into a single movie. These include things from the original trilogy and Han, Chewie, and Lando's backstory. They could have picked just a couple, rather than putting them all into the one script.
