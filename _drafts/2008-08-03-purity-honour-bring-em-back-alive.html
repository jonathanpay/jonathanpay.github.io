“I want to bring [them] back from the grave and have them as a part of my character in life.”

I’ve left the Ranch. I’ve packed only what I need to keep me going on my adventure to find purity and honour. I think I’m getting better at being an Adult and with the right help from others around me I can only improve even more.

I’m concentrating on achieving purity at the moment. Not that I’ll ever reach ‘pure’, however. Purity of thought is more what I’m looking for - To focus on wholesome thoughts and discard anything else. I can’t go anywhere in life if I’m just digging an over sized hole in the ground for myself.

I think I’ve found the right motivation to help me grow up, slim down and get out there. I’m thanking God for her.

I had a crush on a girl, we dated for about 2 months. I did, however, find my someone many years later and she really was, and continues to be, the right person for me and I thank God for her.
