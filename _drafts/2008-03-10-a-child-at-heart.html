There comes a time in everyone's life where they become self-sufficient, at this point they both want and are encouraged to move out of their Parent's home. I'm not personally at that stage just yet, but I'll get there. I feel I've still got a lot of maturing to do.

This morning, graciously, God raised my eyes to him. "What is age to God, the Ancient of Days?" was the resulting question after about thirty seconds of thought hopping. Seconds, minutes, hours, days, weeks, months, seasons, years, decades, millennia… the answer: Nothing.

In the beginning, God created the heavens and the earth…And God said, Let there be lights in the expanse of the heavens to separate the day from the night. And let them be for signs and for seasons, and for days and years.

Genesis 1:1,14

God was before creation, I do not care to speculate how long creation took or what was before creation, I take God's word as is in this department of theology. It is not important to know how or why, just that it is. Much like how Moses, when writing Genesis, decided that the hows were not as important as what the result was. I'm a little off course, back to my point. To God, no matter what our age - a few hours to 119 years - we are all God's children. In him we are encouraged to be 'child like in faith' drawing into God, longing and embracing God's presence as God, like any good parent, longs for ours.

This is a really good spot for me to come in and say yes I held this view. The details that I had at the time informed my theology I have grown since then. That weird nonsense stance of not wanting to speculate how long creation took or what was before creation is very odd to me now.

To be clear, being childlike in Faith, drawing near to God, I'm all for but I have a better, stronger, and deeper understanding of the Bible now. The historical context, around the way certain things were written, and how I'm in this space of reconstructin my faith.
