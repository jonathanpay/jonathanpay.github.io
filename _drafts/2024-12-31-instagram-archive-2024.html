Having disabled my Instagram account, but not before downloading a copy of everything, here is a select archive from 2024...

26 January 2024 – Thank you, dad. I wish A got to know you better, but I’m so grateful for what short time you did spend with her. Even if you did give us all covid at the time 🤣

Feb 11, 2024 – Stories from the Seafront

14 February 2024 – I have been sat today with the realisation (too little too late) that Israel have achieved by most measures what they set out to do at the cost of all of those Palestinian lives. They now no longer have homes to go to. The Gaza strip has been levelled. They have no way to rebuild and so the forceful occupation of the Palestinian lands by the state of Israel will continue and expand. I am angry, I am heartbroken I am devastated. I am horrified for the people of Palestine. Whatever consequences Israel faces after this, I do not think we’ll be enough enough. I also think that we need to be mindful of the West Bank and what they will do after they have finished in Gaza.

8 March 2024 – 4 years ago today. In hospital, recovering from having a brain stem haemorrhage. They release me from hospital a couple weeks after this just before the UK went into lockdown. The initial 2.5 weeks of the 4+ weeks in hospital I cannot recall at all. I’ve no memory of it. I was the funniest I’ve been. By this point I was remembering days in the hospital, probably wasn’t as funny anymore. Trying to deal with double vision that was very unfocused and a lot harder to work with then, though still remains a nuisance…

13 March 2024 – I won too many tokens today at the arcade. I was too amazed at the number of tickets spewing out I didn’t think to collect and fold at the time (also has toddler with me who wanted to help)

4 May 2024

30 November 2024 – Stars of Time comic con in W-S-M, I met Chewbacca. Fun fact, Peter Mayhew and I share the same birthday.

30 December 2024 – So this year on Boxing Day, we went to the panto! Brilliant idea from Charlotte. Not least because Melanie Walters (Gwen from Gavin & Stacy) was the fairy! Safe to say there were plenty of G&S references, and in the wake of the finale the night before it felt extra cathartic. The Dame even dresses up as Nessa for a scene… The panto itself was good, too - I just got caught up in the G&S hype…
