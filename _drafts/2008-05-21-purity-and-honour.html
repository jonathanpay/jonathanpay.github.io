Thought I'd try something a little different. I woke on the 19th, it felt no different to any other day: I felt no change, I felt no older or mature. I woke as a 21 year old: a day older than the day before and a year older than that time last year, some people would be excited. Me, it was just another day. I find birthdays overrated. Saying that, perhaps it's my recent disappointment with myself that has lead me to this understanding. Just the day before, I was baptised - again I felt no different, I'm finding Christianity harder and harder to live with. I'm not about to quit it, I'm just struggling. Moving on.

Well, now I'm a 21 year old Christian who has never been in a relationship, despite my desire to find someone. Perhaps I've been looking in the wrong places, at the wrong people, at the wrong time - Or, I've not looked hard enough. I have established that I have a (surprise, surprise) fear of rejection. This has made many, if not all, attempts to ask a girl out either not happen, happen too late or at the wrong time. Unlucky me. Maybe I should give up? But where is the fun in that?

Perhaps I should take a long vacation? Wait, thats what I'm planning!

I'm looking to go to Sydney for at least a month to kick back and chill with my Old Skool High School friends. It will be great to see them and Sydney again. Pancakes @ the Rocks here I come.

Back on the subject of Purity and Honour.

Honour seems to be some lost forgotten virtue that is only mentioned in Samurai movies. Well, like chivalry, I want to bring it back from the grave and have them as a part of my character in life.

Thats kinda all I have for the moment...

Hoo boy, recovering neckbeard spotting some of those flags in old blog posts. Off the pages of this blog I did eventually chill on the whole finding a relationship thing enough to accept being single for a good while.

Those few times I've gone back to Australia I have had good but sometimes odd times catching up with old friends. They were frozen in my mind as I left them and catching up was a wild story sometimes.
