For a while there I didn't watch TV. I just didn't sit down and watch what was showing on Sky. I did however watch some of my box sets.

There are a few TV shows that I really enjoy and I enjoy them so much more when I don't have to watch an episode a week. I can just sit down and in an evening watch, from start to finish, a whole season of a show like Entourage or Family Guy/American Dad - Or I could take a few more evenings and take in the meatier shows like The Shield or Rome.

I bought a movie on DVD a while back, Event Horizon. I had never seen it but I remember having a poster for it in my room as a younger teen. So I watched it - I watched it all the way through to teach myself a lesson. ALWAYS READ THE BACK OF THE DVD THOROUGHLY - At first I didn't know what to think of it. It was about spaceships and wormholes. A nice bit of sci-fi lightly sprinkled over a horribly demonic story line with ghastly visions of a kind of hell.

I wasn't pleased with myself at all. After the credits started to roll I turned it off and put it back in its case. I'll never watch it again, but I won't throw it out, it will forever serve as a reminder to be a little more discerning about the DVDs I pick. I basically felt that I needed to repent after watching it, if only someone has told me to steer away from it or I'd read more in to it first.

On a better note, I'm starting to compile a list of box sets that I'd like to buy, a good ol' fashioned 'wish list'. With the economy the way it is right now, retailers going in to administration and all I'm hoping to see a drop in some prices of the following DVDs:

Band Of Brothers (bought and watched)
Entourage (bought and watched)
Futurama (bought and watched)
Law & Order SVU (never bought, but watched)
Mad Men (bought but never watched)
The Sopranos (bought but never watched)
Spooks (never bought or watched)
Stargate SG-1 + movies (never bought, but watched)
Stargate Atlantis (never bought, but watched)
Studio 60 On The Sunset Strip (never bought, but watched)
The West Wing (bought but not entirely watched)
The Wire (bought and watched)

There are more, these are just the big ones that I'd like to get.

Edit, 2/2/22: I did watch Event Horizon a second time, not so long after I originally wrote this blog. I had more of an eye for the production of the movie on the second watch and had a better appreciation for the movie's sets and effects, but it still gave me the willies. I have yet to watch it a third time.
