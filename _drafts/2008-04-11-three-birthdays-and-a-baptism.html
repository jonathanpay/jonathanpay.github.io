Its that period of the year where our family organize one birthday after another for 3 months. My youngest sister turned 15 in March, my other sister turns 19 later this month and I hit 21 in May. As well as that I'm also getting baptised the day before my birthday. Over all I'm not sure what I should do for my birthday celebrations.

I'll have been breathing for 21 years. Thats kind of a big deal. Legally, in the USA I'll be able to drink, gamble and so on. Thats not a priority for me, all the same it's nice to know I can. So for my Birthday do I do what I've done for the last 8 years? A movie and/or a BBQ? Or do I go all out and give it a specific theme? If so, what theme should I pick? heroes & Villains? Guys & Dolls? Black & White? Good & Evil?

As you can see I'm not very good at organising parties, too many options overwhelm me.
