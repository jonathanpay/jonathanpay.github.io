(so I'm not actually talking about tweets within tweets, within tweets, but 'tweetception' sounds good off the back of the Movie)

Have you ever come across tweetception? I have (a couple times.)

It happens like this: some savvy business user has set up any number of automated services among their collection of twitter accounts so that one account is setup to tweet various blogs you read and another that automatically retweets those blog tweets. If not organised properly, it can get messy.

So for the sake of your followers and your reputation may I present the following suggestions:

1) Stop all the automated services! List them and reassess their worth with some pros and cons. Perhaps you don’t need all of them, certainly not all at once - you might find a better way of doing it by cutting out some superfluous processes.

2) Don’t use a computer to do a persons job. This is your twitter account! People have chosen to follow you on twitter for what you say and what you share. Bots are obvious and impersonal - you want to be constantly connecting and engaging with your followers. If you do use an autoresponder for new followers, make it personal by not including links, saying that you do check your account regularly so they can tweet you, and so on. 

3) If your Facebook Page or personally account is linked to Twitter, be aware that your post will be shortened to a link automatically. This can lead to issues when your status includes a link already: the fb.me/shortlink automatically forwards a twitter follower to the external link, not the Facebook page with the original post.

This was just a few suggestions for the time being, I will continue to add more in time but I hope that you have found these suggestions helpful!
