The lost art of handwriting. I've picked up my pen over the last few days in an effort to study for my coming exam on Tuesday. Am I prepared for it? Probably not, but thats not something I'm particularly worried about. I've found it both mentally and physically exhausting writing page after page of notes in an effort to hammer this information into my head.

My handwriting is terrible, in an age where Email and SMS have all but killed handwritten letters/notes/cards this has become almost acceptable. There is something special about handwriting something to another person that has become some kind of lost language.

Am I just repeating things that have already been said? Yeah, I probably am. I don't care. While I'm in the subject of handwriting; I used to know Hiragana (one form of Japanese handwriting) But I've forgotten it. Shame really.
