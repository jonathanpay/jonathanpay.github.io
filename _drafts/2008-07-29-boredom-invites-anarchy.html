I've deleted this Space once before. I moved on. I skipped the MySpace fad and jumped straight in to the wholesome goodness of Facebook, I even have a blogger account. I can't se myself keeping this as an up-to-date personal space, I just thought I'd blast a few words on this page for the heck of it.

I'm currently at work with no work to work on, slurping down my third coffee of the day at 5pm. In titling this 'Boredom Invites Anarchy', I am not saying that this is me at my most chaotic-lawlessness (I'm not sure if anarchic is a word.) I do, however, feel the need to do something out of the ordinary. Something, outrageous. A not-so-outrageous thought that I've been having lately is to go camping. I don't mean camping with all the modern comforts either - I want to escape my modern comforts for a weekend. I'm talking about camping with the clothes on my back, a sleeping bag, billy, a couple cans of food and a knife. Maybe a couple matches. Unfortunately (or fortunately?) I'm living in England and they are lacking in wilderness, at least locally they are. I figure I'll set myself up in a patch of woods in some unsuspecting farmer's land. It'll be just me, a (the) good book and God for a weekend.

I want to do this alone because if I invited anyone else along the modern comforts would sneak their way in and the God-and-I time would turn into God-me-and-someone-else time. This idea has been on my heart for a few weeks now and having started to read Wild at Heart by John Eldredge I'm really feeling the urge to go out and conquer my surroundings.

While browsing iStockphoto.com (as you do) I came across a great image of a dusty Bible with "READ ME!" etched in the dust with a finger. I think it is a great image and serves as a wonderful reminder.

Cheers big ears.
