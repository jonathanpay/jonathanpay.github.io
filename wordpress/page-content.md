## About this email marketing maturity assessment

Most email programmes are strong in some areas and quietly held back in others. This free assessment shows you which is which. It looks at how your programme normally works today, rather than at your own skills, and gives you a profile across five areas with a practical next step.

It takes 15–20 minutes. There's no sign-up, and you see your result straight away.

## The five areas it covers

**Strategy & Governance.** Whether the programme has clear priorities, someone accountable for it, and a habit of turning reviews into action.

**Deliverability & Infrastructure.** How permission is gained and recorded, how your list is kept healthy, who looks after the technical setup, and whether you know your emails reach the inbox.

**Customer Experience & Psychology.** How well you understand the people you email, and the quality of your copy, design and accessibility.

**Testing & Learning.** How you choose what to test, how you judge the results, and whether what you learn builds up over time.

**Personalisation & Lifecycle.** How relevant your emails are to each audience, how they support people at each stage of their relationship with you, and how your messages are coordinated.

Each area is placed on one of five maturity levels: Ad Hoc, Foundational, Strategic, Optimised and Innovative.

## How it works

1. Choose one email programme you know reasonably well.
2. Answer three short questions about your role and organisation, then 17 questions about what normally happens now.
3. If you can't answer a question, choose "I don't know". Your result shows which areas to check with a colleague or provider.
4. See your profile straight away: what to keep doing, what to fix first, and a useful next step. You can print it, save it as a PDF or have it emailed to you.

## Who it's for

Anyone who works on or with an email programme: marketing and CRM teams, managers responsible for email, and agencies or consultants looking at a client's programme. It works for ecommerce, B2B, subscription, charity and public-sector programmes alike.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the assessment free?

Yes. There's no charge and no sign-up.

### Do I need to give my email address?

No. You only enter your name and email if you'd like a copy of your result sent to you.

### What happens to my answers?

When you finish, your answers and result are saved by Holistic Email Academy to improve this assessment and our courses. Our [privacy policy](/privacy-policy/) explains how we look after them.

### Can I take it again?

Yes, at any time. Take it again after making changes, or for a different programme. You can also download your answers and open them on this page later.

### What is an email marketing maturity model?

It's a way of describing how developed an email programme is, from inconsistent and reactive to planned, measured and continually improving. This assessment uses five areas and five levels, so you can see where your programme is strong and where it's being held back.

### What should I do with my result?

Start with the next step in your result. If you want to build skills in a particular area, [Holistic Email Academy's courses](/courses/) cover all five.
